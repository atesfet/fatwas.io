Phenome-wide Associations of CT-derived Adipose Area and Radiodensity in 22,779 Patients: Uncovering Unexpected Disease Connections

FatWAS Study Team
Code

Abstract

Large-scale, quantitative phenotyping from already-acquired clinical imaging offers a new opportunistic avenue to characterize adipose tissue. Here, we perform a phenome-wide association study (PheWAS) of adipose composition derived automatically from abdominal CT scans.

We linked subcutaneous and visceral fat area (SFI and VFI) and radiodensity (SFD and VFD) from 22,779 patients to 1,447 EHR-derived phenotypes. We identified 1,563 significant associations across 522 diseases, recapitulating known links and identifying novel associations between adipose radiodensity and systemic disease.

This interactive website provides Manhattan and volcano plots for each marker and a searchable table of all significant associations.

Website Usage Tips

Hover over points for phenotype details and confidence intervals.

Use drag/zoom tools in the top-right of each plot to inspect dense regions.

Use the Focus button in the table to activate phenotype focus view across all plots.

In focus view, all associated markers are highlighted; non-associated marker plots show a centered \"not significant\" message.

Use Download Focus PDF to export a report with focused plots and the phenotype's associated rows.

Use Open Discussion (GitHub) to open the phenotype discussion thread/search in this repository (GitHub account required to comment). This is not an "Issue" but a "Feature"!
